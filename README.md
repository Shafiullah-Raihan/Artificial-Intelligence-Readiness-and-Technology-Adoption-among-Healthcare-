
# Artificial Intelligence Readiness and Technology Adoption among Healthcare Professionals

### Peer-Reviewed Journal Article | Artificial Intelligence | Healthcare | Technology Adoption

## 📌 Overview

This repository contains a peer-reviewed research article titled **"Artificial Intelligence Readiness and Technology Adoption among Healthcare Professionals"**, along with associated research data and supporting documents.

The research explores artificial intelligence (AI) readiness and technology adoption in the healthcare sector, focusing on the factors that influence the acceptance and integration of emerging AI technologies in healthcare environments.

The study contributes to the growing body of research on AI adoption, technological readiness, and digital transformation in healthcare.

## 🎯 Research Objectives

The research focuses on the following areas:

- Investigating artificial intelligence readiness in healthcare.
- Exploring factors influencing AI technology adoption.
- Understanding the relationship between technological readiness and technology acceptance.
- Identifying potential challenges to AI implementation.
- Contributing to research on digital transformation and healthcare innovation.

## 🔬 Research Focus

The key research areas include:

- **Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare:** The role of AI in healthcare environments.
- **Technology Adoption:** Factors influencing the acceptance and use of emerging technologies.
- **AI Readiness:** Preparedness for adopting and integrating AI-based systems.
- **Healthcare Digital Transformation:** The integration of modern technologies into healthcare practices.

## 🌍 Research Significance

Artificial intelligence is increasingly influencing healthcare systems, making technological readiness and adoption important areas of research.

Understanding these factors can contribute to:

- Better understanding of AI acceptance in healthcare.
- Identification of potential barriers to technology adoption.
- Improved awareness of organisational and professional AI readiness.
- Future research on AI-driven healthcare transformation.
